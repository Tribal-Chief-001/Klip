Remove a linked device (by name or by the start of its fingerprint), or all of them with --all

Usage: klip unpair [OPTIONS] [DEVICE]

Arguments:
  [DEVICE]  Name or start of the fingerprint of the device to remove

Options:
      --all   Remove every linked device
  -y, --yes   Do not ask for confirmation (with --all)
  -h, --help  Print help
